About Atachyon

We started Atachyon with a broader observation that the nature of threats to national security is changing. Critical infrastructure — ports, energy facilities, airports and other strategic assets — is becoming increasingly exposed to conventional and emerging unmanned and autonomous threats.

Existing surveillance infrastructure is largely dependent on fragmented sensors and human monitoring. Standalone counter-drone/UAV technology falls short on subsurface threats and perimeter security. We believe the next generation of infrastructure protection needs to be unified, autonomous and intelligence-led.

We are developing an AI-powered EO/IR payload integrated with radar technology to deliver persistent land, air and water situational awareness through a unified manner across complex geographies. An in-house system-on-chip (SoC) architecture enables on-device processing, effectively bringing the intelligence of a control room into the sensing platform itself. An AI agent continuously analyses sensor data at the edge, relaying actionable alerts to security teams instead of relying on continuous video streaming and human monitoring.

The system is designed for rapid deployment, grid-independent operation and integration with existing soft-kill and hard-kill countermeasure systems.

We are currently developing our integrated sensor payload and working towards TRL 3. We have two major commercial infrastructure operators in our pilot pipeline, representing approximately 1,700 acres of prospective coverage and ₹5,000 crore in combined asset value.
