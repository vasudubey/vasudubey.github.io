About Atachyon

We started Atachyon in 2026 with a broader observation that the nature of threats to national security is changing. Critical infrastructure — ports, energy facilities, airports and other strategic assets — is becoming increasingly exposed to conventional and emerging unmanned and autonomous threats.

Existing surveillance infrastructure is largely dependent on fragmented sensors and human monitoring. Standalone counter-drone/UAS systems primarily address aerial threats, leaving significant gaps in terrestrial intrusion detection and integrated perimeter security. We believe the next generation of infrastructure protection needs to be unified, autonomous and intelligence-led.

We are building a proprietary payload integrating radar, EO/IR imaging and edge AI to deliver situational awareness for land, air and water in a unified manner across complex geographies. The payload carries its own onboard AI, which continuously monitors and analyses sensor data at the edge. This eliminates the need for a separate, continuously staffed control room or endless video feeds. The system autonomously identifies events of interest and sends only relevant alerts and information to backend security teams

The payload is designed for mast-mounted deployment at protected sites, with rapid installation, grid-independent operation and integration capabilities for existing soft-kill and hard-kill countermeasure systems.

We aim to create a national intelligence grid that makes persistent situational awareness reproducible at scale.

We are currently developing our integrated sensor payload and working towards TRL 3. We have two major port operators in our pilot pipeline, representing approximately 1,700 acres of prospective coverage and ₹5,000 crore in combined asset value.
