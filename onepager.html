Overview
  • The nature of threats to national security is changing.
  • Autonomous war will be fought at critical infrastructure, not just borders.
Problem
  • Existing surveillance relies on fragmented sensors and human monitoring.
  • Standalone counter-drone/UAS systems address only aerial threats, leaving gaps in terrestrial intrusion detection and integrated perimeter security.
  • Existing solutions fall short on three counts: fragmented coverage, human dependency and high operating cost.
The GapConventional surveillance systems cannot provide unified, persistent situational awareness for land, air and water through a single system across complex geographies.
Our Solution
  • Proprietary payload integrating radar, EO/IR imaging and edge AI for unified situational awareness across land, air and water in complex geographies.
  • Onboard AI continuously monitors and analyses sensor data at the edge, removing the need for a staffed control room or endless video feeds.
  • Autonomously identifies events of interest and sends only relevant alerts to backend security teams.

Impact: Transforms passive surveillance into autonomous threat detection, reducing human dependency while enabling continuous monitoring across land, air and water.

USP
  • Unified 3-in-1 coverage with no backend control room
  • Easy integration of soft kill / hard kill systems
  • Rapidly deployable and grid independent
  • In-house design with real-time Edge AI
