I'm Vasu, 23. I grew up in Rewa and now live in Bangalore, where I'm the founder and CEO of Atachyon. We're building autonomous systems to protect critical and strategic industrial assets.

I've been building since I was 13. My first venture was Velour, an online business directory for my hometown, Rewa, that helped people connect and engage with local businesses digitally.

I studied engineering at SGSITS, but dropped out after the first semester to build Resonance, a machine learning-based medical device focused on speech restoration for laryngectomy patients. It was backed by American economist Tyler Cowen.

I grew up in a family of engineers and army officers, so I was around technology from a young age. My frequent visits to the Ordnance Factory in Ambernath are where my love for defence products began. I went to the fairs and expos they organised and talked with the engineers building sovereign defence technology for my country. Atachyon grew out of that curiosity.

Emergent Ventures - I am a Mercatus Center Fellow, awarded by George Mason University, USA.
